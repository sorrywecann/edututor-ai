## Scenario 12 — **VOICE LOOP** (THE critical bug)

This is the bug we're chasing. Do this carefully and capture the Console output.

**Setup:**
1. Open DevTools: **Ctrl+Shift+I** → click **Console** tab
2. Right-click in Console → **Clear console** (so output starts fresh)
3. Have a paper/note ready to write the timing

**Action:**
1. Click amber orb (orb should turn from idle → listening)
2. Speak: "Aký bude zajtra počasie?"
3. Wait for response to play and finish
4. **DO NOT click anywhere.** Wait 10 seconds.
5. **Expected:** orb should AUTOMATICALLY return to listening state (without you clicking)
6. Once listening, speak: "A pozajtra?"
7. Wait for response.
8. Did step 6 work without clicking the orb? YES = PASS / NO = FAIL

**Result:** [ ] PASS  [ ] FAIL
